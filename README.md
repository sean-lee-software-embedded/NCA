# NCA-AIIO Weekend Study Guide

Traditional Chinese study notes for the **NVIDIA-Certified Associate: AI Infrastructure and Operations (NCA-AIIO)** certification.

## Study guide

Read the guide on [GitHub Pages](https://sean-lee-software-embedded.github.io/NCA/), or open [`index.html`](index.html) locally in a web browser. The guide is self-contained and can also be printed to PDF using the browser's print function.

## Topics

- AI, machine learning, deep learning, and generative AI
- Training and inference
- CPU, GPU, and accelerated computing
- NVIDIA software and hardware platforms
- AI networking and storage
- Data-center power and cooling
- Kubernetes, GPU Operator, monitoring, and MLOps
- BMC and Redfish for AI infrastructure
- Practice questions and a final review sheet

## Disclaimer

This is an independently prepared study resource. It is not official NVIDIA training material, is not affiliated with or endorsed by NVIDIA, and does not contain or reproduce actual certification exam questions. Product names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.

Certification requirements and product details can change. Verify current information on the [official NVIDIA NCA-AIIO certification page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/learn/certification/ai-infrastructure-operations-associate/) before taking the exam.

## Language

The study guide is written in Traditional Chinese (`zh-Hant`).
